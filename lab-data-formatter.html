Row placeholders: {label} = label without units, {name} = alias for {label}, {unit} = unit, {values} = selected values. Each-value placeholders: {value}, {date}, {unit}. Date tokens: YYYY, MM, DD (zero-padded), M, D (no leading zeroes). Example: M/D → 9/1. Use \n for a newline or \t for a tab. Alignment preserves row-format text and adds padding before and between values. Blank cells are omitted when alignment is off, including when dates are shown. With alignment on and dates hidden, values start at the left; “Align with dates” preserves their original date positions.
